1 /gaps: the gap catalog, as a template

One gap block, repeated with {{ openTag }}. Every value comes from a row. The fields sit in the same order in every block: status band, title, intent, check, held, closes when, gap document, signed. Empty fields keep their place and say they are empty.

Status sets the band and the card edge. Unsigned is red with a red offset. Signed, open is gold with a gold offset and a held list, so it reads as allowed and holding, never as passing. Closed is flat ink. Replaced and invalidated are dashed and dimmed. The status word is always written.

I could not read site/style.css (the repo was not reachable), so this uses the design system tokens the site was built from. Rows marked Sample data are invented. Tweaks: show the Mustache field names, or fill the five-status page with 30 rows.

Gaps

A gap is a place where a check fails and we know it. Its document says which sentence of the intent is not yet met, which check proves it, and what closes it.

A signed gap lets the build finish. It never lets anything ship: every step that ships stays held until the check returns 0.
