# Drift King: gap

Our signed intent says: "The model is the only place facts live"
https://drift-king.org/intent/letter-of-intent#:~:text=The%20model%20is%20the%20only%20place%20facts%20live

We have seen that the model cannot say what Drift King is working on. Each agreement we sign asks for work, but the model does not say what that work is or when it is done. So which agreements are still open is worked out by hand, in conversation, and it gets missed.

We have also seen that a signing can be completed in DocuSign and never reach ah. Nothing notices, so the model believes the agreement was never signed.

To close this, each agreement in ah says what it asks for and what fulfils it. A query over ah then answers which agreements are open, and the console's Intents screen shows that answer. A second check compares the signings in DocuSign with the signings in ah, and a canary with a signing missing from ah must fail it.

What we do not do: we do not keep a status by hand, and we do not trust a list that the model did not produce.

Signed: Drift King Compliance
