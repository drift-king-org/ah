# Drift King: gap

Our signed intent says: "Behavior, tests, code and the model are projections of that intent."
https://drift-king.org/intent/letter-of-intent#:~:text=Behavior%2C%20tests%2C%20code%20and%20the%20model%20are%20projections%20of%20that%20intent.

We have seen that an agreement cannot go live on its own. drift-king.org ships as one upload, so publishing an agreement also ships every projection committed since the last ship, whether or not it was OK'd. A signing then waits on unrelated work, or unrelated work ships to get a signing out.

To close this, agreements and projections ship separately. Publishing an agreement uploads only the agreement, its approval page and what they link, at the addresses already signed. A check proves the upload holds nothing else, and a canary with one projection in it must fail that check.

What we do not do: we do not ship a projection to publish an agreement, and we do not move a signed address.
