# x-components: what Drift King asks of the next version

The x-components letter says: "Drift King uses it. Anyone may."
https://drift-king.org/intent/x-components-letter-of-intent#:~:text=Drift%20King%20uses%20it.%20Anyone%20may.

Drift King is writing drift-king.org in x-components. Every page that is not a signed page is to be made of components and nothing else. Version 0.1.0 has most of what the site needs. It does not yet have four things.

We ask for four components, each generic, with no words of any one site in them. A ticker: a band of short phrases that scrolls, and stands still for anyone who asks for less motion. Paired line panes: two numbered lists of lines side by side, where one line can be marked and a stamp can sit across a pane. A sticker: a short label, set at an angle over an image. A seal: a round mark holding a line or two of text, set beside a block of text.

We also ask for two changes to what good looks like. The classes a component emits are part of the contract: the model names them, a check proves the code emits exactly those, and renaming one is a new major version. And no visible words are defaults: every word a component shows comes from the site that uses it.

Each addition comes with its checks and canaries, like every line of the list, and ships in a new version. Drift King takes that version only after it audits it.

What we do not do: we do not ask for anything only Drift King could use. What goes into x-components is for anyone.

Signed: Drift King Compliance
