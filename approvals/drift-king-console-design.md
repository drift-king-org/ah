# Drift King: console design

Our signed console agreement says: "The console is built from x-components and the model, from a design the designer sends and we sign separately."
https://drift-king.org/approvals/console#:~:text=The%20console%20is%20built%20from%20x%2Dcomponents%20and%20the%20model%2C%20from%20a%20design%20the%20designer%20sends%20and%20we%20sign%20separately.

The designer sent a design for the console, as one zip. It has the Intents screen, Intents in every state, and Watch a build.
https://drift-king.org/design/drift-king-console-design.zip

Its approval page lists it with its fingerprint.
https://drift-king.org/approvals/drift-king-console-design-approval

This approves building the console from that design. The console is built from x-components and the model, not copied from the zip. The design names eight components that x-components does not have yet, and they come to x-components first, each signed in its own release.

What we do not do: we do not ship the design's HTML as it is, and we do not show any sample from it as if the model said it.

Signed: Drift King Compliance
