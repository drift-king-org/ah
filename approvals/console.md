# Drift King: the console

Our signed intent says: "The model is the only place facts live"
https://drift-king.org/intent/letter-of-intent#:~:text=The%20model%20is%20the%20only%20place%20facts%20live

We signed a gap saying the model can't tell which agreements are open.
https://drift-king.org/approvals/gap-open-agreements

This builds the console at drift-king.org/console, so the answer is on a page and not in a conversation. Its first screen is Intents:

- every agreement in ah, with what it asks for and what fulfils it
- each one open or done, as a query over ah works it out
- every open gap, with the sentence of the letter it answers

The console is built from x-components and the model, from a design the designer sends and we sign separately. When it opens, the links to /console on the landing page open too.

What we do not do: we do not keep a status by hand, and we do not show anything the model did not produce.
